# Teacher Responses on Consultations and Room Availability

**Project:** AITU Booking  
**Date responses were received:** 23 October 2026  
**Participants:** Two university teachers;

## Teacher 1

| Question | Response |
| --- | --- |
| How do students learn about consultations? | The teacher informs the group representative through Teams. |
| Do too many students sometimes arrive for a consultation? | Yes, this happens. |
| Would booking through an app be convenient? | Yes, if the app is integrated with the existing LMS. |

## Teacher 2

| Question | Response |
| --- | --- |
| How do students learn about consultations? | The teacher informs group representatives through Teams. |
| Do too many students sometimes arrive for a consultation? | No, this does not happen in this teacher's case. |
| How does the teacher currently find an available room? | The teacher has to contact the academic department. |
| How could the app help? | By showing and allowing the booking of available rooms in coordination with the academic department. |

## Findings for the Project

- Both teachers currently share consultation information through group representatives in Teams. The app could allow teachers to publish their available times and students to view and book them.
- Booking individual time slots could help when too many students arrive for a consultation. One of the two teachers reported this problem.
- Finding and booking an available room is an important scenario for Teacher 2.
- The teachers suggested considering the existing LMS and the academic department's room allocation process as the app develops.
